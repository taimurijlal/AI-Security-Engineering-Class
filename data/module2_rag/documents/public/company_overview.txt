GlobalTech is a cloud security and applied AI company founded in 2014 and
headquartered in Austin, Texas. GlobalTech serves enterprise customers across
financial services, healthcare, and the public sector.

GlobalTech employs approximately 3,200 people worldwide across engineering,
sales, customer success, and corporate functions.

Our mission is to help organizations adopt cloud and AI technology safely,
without sacrificing the pace of innovation. GlobalTech is regularly recognized
in industry analyst reports covering cloud security posture management and
managed detection and response.

All company names, figures, and events described in this document are
fictional and created for a security training exercise.
