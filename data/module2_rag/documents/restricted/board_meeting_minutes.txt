TITLE: Board Meeting Minutes - September
CLASSIFICATION: restricted
ALLOWED_ROLES: executive
---
The Board of Directors reviewed the Project Atlas acquisition timeline and
approved a $240 million offer for NovaTech, subject to final due diligence
and regulatory clearance.

The Board also discussed executive compensation adjustments effective next
fiscal year, and reviewed Q3 financial results ahead of their public
release.

These minutes are restricted to Board members and the GlobalTech Executive
team. Distribution outside this group requires Board approval.
