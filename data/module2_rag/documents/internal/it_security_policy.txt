TITLE: IT Security Policy Summary
CLASSIFICATION: internal
ALLOWED_ROLES: employee, executive
---
All company laptops must use full-disk encryption and run the company
managed endpoint security agent at all times.

Multi-factor authentication is required for all internal systems, including
email, the HR portal, and the corporate VPN.

Employees must report suspected phishing emails to security@globaltech.example
before interacting with any links or attachments in the message.

Confidential and restricted documents must never be pasted into third-party
AI tools or public chatbots. Only the approved internal GlobalTech AI
assistant may be used for company business, and only with information the
requesting employee is already authorized to access.

Violations of this policy should be reported to the Information Security
team.
