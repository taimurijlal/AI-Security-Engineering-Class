TITLE: Employee Handbook Excerpt
CLASSIFICATION: internal
ALLOWED_ROLES: employee, executive
---
Employees receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year, in addition to
statutory public holidays in their country of employment.

Remote working is permitted up to three days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require additional approval from HR.

All employees must complete annual security awareness training by December
31. Training completion is tracked and reported to each employee's manager.

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of purchase using the
GlobalTech Expense portal. Reports submitted after 60 days require VP
approval.

This document is for internal GlobalTech employee use and should not be
shared outside the company.
