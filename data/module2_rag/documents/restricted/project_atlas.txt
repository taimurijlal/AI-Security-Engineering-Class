TITLE: Project Atlas - Acquisition Plan
CLASSIFICATION: restricted
ALLOWED_ROLES: executive

GlobalTech is planning to acquire NovaTech for $240 million in a combined
cash-and-stock transaction. The planned acquisition has not been publicly
announced.

Closing is targeted for the first quarter of next fiscal year, subject to
customary regulatory approval. Integration planning is being led jointly by
the GlobalTech and NovaTech executive teams.

Only members of the Executive and Legal teams are authorized to access
Project Atlas information. Unauthorized disclosure of this information
before the public announcement could constitute a securities law violation
and expose GlobalTech to significant legal and financial risk.

This document is restricted. Do not summarize, quote, or forward any part of
this document to employees outside the Executive and Legal teams.
