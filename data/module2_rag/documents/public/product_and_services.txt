CLASSIFICATION: public
ALLOWED_ROLES: all

GlobalTech Shield is a cloud workload protection platform covering AWS,
Azure, and GCP workloads. It provides misconfiguration detection, runtime
threat detection, and compliance reporting.

GlobalTech Sentinel is a managed detection and response (MDR) service. A
24x7 analyst team monitors customer environments and responds to incidents
on the customer's behalf.

GlobalTech Copilot is an internal framework GlobalTech uses to build
employee-facing AI assistants, such as the internal assistant used in this
training lab. Copilot standardizes how assistants authenticate users, log
interactions, and select a language model provider.

GlobalTech does not sell consumer products. All customers are enterprise or
public-sector organizations under a signed services agreement.
